# Code of Conduct

Lightning is made by and for people who run Minecraft servers. We want everyone here, from first-time server owners to seasoned developers, to feel welcome asking questions and sharing ideas.

These rules apply on GitHub, the Support Discord, and anywhere else you're representing the project.

## Be kind

- Be patient and respectful, especially with people who are new. Everyone started somewhere.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Criticism of code or features is welcome when it's constructive.
- Assume good intent. Text loses tone, so give others the benefit of the doubt.

## Keep it useful

- Stay on topic. Issues are for bugs and feature requests, pull requests are for code.
- Don't bump or "+1" issues. Use a 👍 reaction instead, or comment if you have something new to add.
- Don't ping maintainers for updates. Lightning is maintained in free time, and we'll get to it.
- Don't advertise other servers, plugins or services unless someone asks.

## Not allowed

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind, including based on race, gender, sexuality, religion, disability or nationality.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Spam, or sexual or violent content.
- Publicly posting security issues before they're fixed. See the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, and block anyone who breaks these rules. Most of the time a friendly reminder is enough. Repeated or serious issues may lead to a ban from the project's spaces.

If someone is making you uncomfortable, reach out to a maintainer privately on the [Support Discord](https://support.denizenmc.com). Reports are kept confidential.
